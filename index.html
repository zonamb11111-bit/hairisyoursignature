Common questions answered

Yes, HIKARU TERADA is an English-speaking hair salon in Tokyo. The salon specializes in curly, wavy, and Western hair textures that are often poorly handled at standard Japanese salons. Hikaru Terada has 15+ years of experience and has worked with clients from over 40 countries, conducting full consultations in fluent English.

The salon is located in Koenji, Suginami-ku, Tokyo — 7 minutes from Shinjuku on the JR Chuo line with no transfer. It operates as a one-on-one private studio, meaning no other clients are present during your appointment.

Booking is online only through the integrated reservation form on the website, which supports both English and Japanese in a single flow. Real-time availability is shown directly from the salon's calendar. Free pre-consultations via Instagram DM or email are recommended for color or major changes. Walk-ins are not accepted.
